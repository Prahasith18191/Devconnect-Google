# 2-Minute Demo Script: DevConnect AI
> Live Pitch & Technical Demo for Build with AI Developer Bootcamp, Hyderabad

---

## 🎯 Hook (0:00 - 0:20)
> *"Hello everyone! We are at this amazing AI bootcamp in Hyderabad with 250+ incredible developers, mentors, and founders. But the hardest problem isn't building the model—it's meeting the right person building with your exact stack, remembering their details, and staying connected after the final keynote. Today, I built **DevConnect AI** to solve that."*

---

## 🔍 Act 1: Technical Interest Matchmaking (0:20 - 0:50)
> *"Here is our Event Discovery screen. Instead of generic LinkedIn browsing, DevConnect AI is tailored for technical builders. I filter by 'FastAPI', 'Gemini 2.5', and 'Vector Databases'.*
> *Instantly, our matching engine highlights attendees who share these exact interests. Here's Priya, a Senior Backend Engineer building multimodal agents. I can see her GitHub, current projects, and exactly what she's learning today."*

---

## 🤝 Act 2: High-Context Connection Request & Notifications (0:50 - 1:15)
> *"I click 'Connect', and add a quick 1-line note: 'Saw your vector search repo—would love to chat at lunch!'*
> *The system securely persists this in Supabase, triggers an in-app notification, and dispatches a transactional SMTP email alert. Once accepted, an encrypted 1-on-1 direct messaging channel unlocks immediately."*

---

## 💬 Act 3: Live Lounge & Community Feed (1:15 - 1:40)
> *"Meanwhile, the physical event has a live pulse. In the Hyderabad Event Lounge, attendees are sharing lunch announcements, hackathon team calls, and breakout table locations. On the Community Feed, builders share their live prototypes with single-reaction likes and code-snippet comments."*

---

## 🛡️ Act 4: Architecture & Admin Control (1:40 - 2:00)
> *"Under the hood: React 19 and Tailwind on the frontend, Python FastAPI on the backend with strict Pydantic v1 contracts, and Supabase PostgreSQL with rigorous Row Level Security. Admins can update the event venue, manage agenda sessions, and moderate spam in real time.*
> *This is DevConnect AI: One event. Many builders. Meaningful connections. Thank you!"*
